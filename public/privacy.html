Privacy Policy for UniTonight

Last updated: September 2026

Robcore Ltd ("we", "our", or "us") operates the UniTonight mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

1. Information We Collect

When you use UniTonight, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Email address, name, profile picture, and user ID provided during account creation.
  • Location Data: With your permission, we collect precise location data to match you with nearby venues and crews.
  • Usage Data: Automatically collected data such as device type, operating system, and App usage statistics.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the App.
  • Manage your account and facilitate crew matching.
  • Serve targeted advertisements through Google AdMob.

3. Third-Party Services

We use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. These include Google Play Services, Firebase Authentication, and Google AdMob.

4. Data Security

Your data is encrypted in transit using industry-standard protocols. We rely on Firebase secure infrastructure to store user profiles.

5. Data Deletion

You have the right to request the deletion of your personal data. You can delete your account directly from within the App settings.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about our Privacy Policy, please contact Robcore Ltd.
